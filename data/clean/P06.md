Monitor Your Home Energy Usage
How to Use Austin Energy Monitoring Tools
One of the best ways to save money and energy is to monitor your electricity use to find out where costs are coming from. Instructions for residential and commercial customers are below.
Log into your COA Utilities account for up-to-date information about your energy use, including:
- Use by day, month and year
- How your usage compares to last year
- How your usage compares to similar homes in your area
- A breakdown of your bill by cooling & heating, appliances, electronics, and other uses
- Solar panel energy production, if you have them
