The City of Austin provides public utilities to residents of Austin and some surrounding areas. Depending on where you live, you could receive one or more utility services from the City of Austin.
Your bill includes charges for all of your City of Austin utility services. It can include charges for drainage, electricity, solid waste, street service, wastewater, and water.
Customer service representatives are available to help at our walk-in utility customer service centers and by telephone. You can also register to manage your account and pay your bill online.

Start, Stop, or Transfer Service
You can start, stop, or transfer utility service with the City of Austin on the phone, online, or in person:
- Call Customer Care at 512-494-9400 to speak with a representative
- Register with Online Customer Care to request new service or changes to your existing service
- Visit one of our walk-in utility customer service centers
Understand Requirements and Options for Fees and Security Deposits
The City of Austin charges fees and security deposits to start utility service. We provide ways to waive deposits, and we refund them with interest when certain conditions are met.
- Get details about residential utility fees and deposits
- Get details about commercial utility fees and deposits
Manage Your Utility Account and Request Past Records
City of Austin utility customers have a variety of options available to them to update and manage access to their utility account, add authorized users, release account information, and request past account records. Also, it's easy to update your address and phone number when needed.

Know Your Confidentiality Options and Customer Rights
Under the Texas Utilities Code, customers have a right to confidentiality in government-operated utilities. Customers can request that their personal information and information related to their volume of utility usage be kept confidential.
