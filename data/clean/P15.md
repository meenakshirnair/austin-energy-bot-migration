Residential Rebates
Upgrade Your Home and Save
Austin Energy's Residential Rebates can help make your home more comfortable and energy efficient. From simple improvements to whole-home energy upgrades, Austin Energy offers many ways to help you save.
Date last reviewed or modified: 02/18/2026

Programs listed on this page:
- Air Conditioning: Earn a rebate averaging $800 to install a new, energy efficient air conditioning system.
- Get Instant Savings on Energy-Efficient Products: Saving money and energy go hand-in-hand when you buy select products at participating local stores.
- Heat Pump Water Heaters: Earn an $1,000 rebate to install an ENERGY STAR® certified Hybrid Electric Water Heater, also referred to as High-Efficiency Heat Pump Water Heater.
- Home Energy Savings: Earn rebates averaging $3,000 and access 0% financing on whole-home energy improvements.
- Home Energy Savings Essential: Access a lower-cost alternative to the standard Home Energy Savings program. Earn rebates averaging $1,500 to upgrade your home to use less energy and lower your electricity bills.
- Pool Pumps: Earn a $300 rebate to install an ENERGY STAR® certified variable-speed pool pump.
- Power Partner Thermostat: Get $50 rebates on smart thermostats and earn up to $75 in bill credits when you enroll in Power Partner.
- Power Partner℠ Battery: Earn rewards averaging $750 when you enroll approved backup batteries in Power Partner Battery.
- Residential Solar Rebate: Earn a $4,000 rebate by completing our solar education course and installing a qualifying solar system on your home.
- Solar Screens: Earn a rebate to install energy-efficient solar screens in your home’s windows.
- Value of Solar Bill Credit: Earn bill credits for energy produced by on-site energy systems.
- Weatherization Assistance: Austin Energy offers free home energy improvements to eligible renters and homeowners. Apply in minutes.
- Window Air Conditioners: Earn a $50 rebate when you buy an ENERGY STAR® certified window air conditioner.
