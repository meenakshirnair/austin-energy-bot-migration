Home EV Charger Rebate
Get up to $500 back on a PowerPartner℠ EV eligible Level 2 home charging station
Austin Energy offers a rebate that covers 50% of the cost (up to $500) for a qualified home charging station—making it easier and more affordable to charge your EV faster at home and help the community manage energy use on days when the need is highest.
Charge at Home with a Level 2 Charging Station
- Faster charging: Level 2 charging is much faster than a standard outlet (120V)
- Start your day fully charged: Skip the gas station and charge overnight
- Give back to the grid: Decrease stress on the electric system while lowering your carbon emissions
Rebate Amounts:
- Up to $500 or 50% of your purchase price for Power Partner℠ EV compatible station. Visit Austin Energy’s Power Partner℠ EV to help you save money and support the grid.
- 
Eligibility Requirements Prerequisites
  - You must receive electricity from Austin Energy.
  - You must have purchased or leased a plug-in electric vehicle.
  - You must enroll in Power Partner℠ EV.
  - You must have a Power Partner EV compatible station; Additional EV chargers paired with a qualifying telematics-enabled vehicle are also eligible.
  - Charging station must be new and installed at applicant's address.
  - You agree to participate in Austin Energy surveys or feedback regarding Austin Energy Electric Vehicle Programs.
  - Applicants are eligible to receive one rebate per Power Partner EV enrolled electric vehicle charging system.
Equipment Installation Requirements
  - All participants are required to ensure that their EV charging station is installed in accordance with all applicable electrical codes and standards.
  - Charging stations and receptacles for plug-in stations must be UL, ETL or cETL listed.
Exclusions
  - Customers must enroll in the PPEV program to receive the rebate and are eligible for one rebate per enrolled device.
- 
How to Apply Apply for Your Rebate Online The Austin Energy account holder must submit the application. Please make sure you have everything you need before starting. The following information is required for your online rebate application: 
  - Active Austin Energy electric customer account number
  - Charging station manufacturer/ model
  - Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
  - Copy of fully paid receipt for charging station
Tips for Applying Online
  - If you choose to submit the online rebate application, you will need to have all required information on hand before you start.
  - Enroll in the Power Partner℠ EV program.
  - You will need to complete and submit the application in a single session. You cannot save your information and start again later. Active sessions time out after one hour of inactivity.
  - You will need to scan or photograph documents and upload into the application system. The system will accept any of the following file formats:
    
    - Adobe Acrobat PDF (.pdf)
    - Microsoft Word Document (.doc or .docx)
    - JPEG (.jpg)
    - ZIP (.zip)
  - Check your rebate status online using the confirmation (enrollment) number you receive after the application submission. You will also need your City of Austin utility account number.
Call us at 512-494-9400 or email pluginaustin@austinenergy.com if you have questions about your rebate status or need help with your rebate application.
