Pay a utility bill
Ways to pay
Pay online
You can pay online with City of Austin Utilities Online Customer Care.
Pay in person or by mail
- Pay in person
- You can pay in person at a Utility Customer Service Center or authorized pay station. See a list of in-person payment locations and hours.
- Pay by mail
- You can pay by mail with a personal check, cashier’s check, or money order. See full instructions for payment by mail.
Payment-related services
You will need to log in to access these payment-related services.
Learn more about all City of Austin Utilities rates and fees and Austin Energy rates and fees.
Customer Assistance Programs
Utility bill discounts are available for customers facing financial difficulties. Learn more about utility bill discounts.
The Medically Vulnerable Registry helps customers with critical medical care needs. Check eligibility requirements for the Medically Vulnerable Registry.
