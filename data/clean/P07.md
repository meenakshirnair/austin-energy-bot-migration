Payment Options
City of Austin Utilities
The City of Austin offers a variety of payment options so you can pay your bill conveniently.
Your payment must be received on or before the due date on the bill.
If you are experiencing financial hardship and are unable to pay your bill, please call City of Austin Utilities Customer Care at 512-494-9400.
Make a Quick Payment Online or By Phone with a Credit Card
Make a payment anytime and enjoy immediate payment posting by utilizing the Quick Payment by phone or online option. Please note there is a convenience fee assessed for each Quick Payment: $3.90 for residential, $10.00 for commercial accounts.
- Call toll-free 1-833-375-4919 to make utility payments by phone
- Or make a Quick Payment online

Pay at an Authorized Pay Station
Pay stations located at stores throughout the community make it convenient to pay your utility bill with cash. The stores may charge a handling fee ($.75 - $2.95) to process payments.
Customers who wish to make a charitable contribution to the Financial Support Plus 1 Fund may do so at Authorized Pay Stations, but will be charged an additional handling fee to process the contribution.
Pay stations provide only bill payment services. Call 512-494-9400 for detailed account information.

Pay at a Drop Box
We accept personal or cashier's checks and money orders at the following drop box locations.
Payments placed in a drop box should include a Bill Payment stub along with payment. Please do not put cash in a drop box.
Payments dropped off outside of business hours are processed on the next business day. Please allow 3-5 business days for drop box payments to be posted.
If you have received a notice of disconnection for non-payment, do not place your payment in a drop box.
- Make your payment online at City of Austin Utilities, at one of our walk-in Utility Customer Service Centers, or at an authorized pay station
- Make a Quick Payment online or call 1-833-375-4919 to make a Quick Payment by phone. One-time Quick Payments will post to your utility account on the same day.
